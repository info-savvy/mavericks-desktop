A table lap dance is an erotic dance performed at the table of a patron. At Mavericks a table dance on the main floor lasts for two songs. Up to four people at a time can enjoy a table dance. At the dancers discretion, additional patrons can join at R 50 per person. Please note that each seated table is required to have either a lap dance or table dance.

A lap dance is an erotic dance or striptease performed on the lap of, or close to a patron. At Mavericks a lap dance on the Main Floor lasts for two songs and can be enjoyed by up to two guests at a time. While the dancer’s attention is for your eyes only, a lap dance on the main floor is only semi-private and may be viewed by other customers close-by. For a more private experience, request a VIP dance. Please note that each seated table is required to have either a lap dance or table dance.

What is a VIP Dance? A VIP Dance at Mavericks is a private erotic dance or striptease performed in a discreet area of a VIP lounge or a luxurious private booth. At Mavericks a VIP dance can be enjoyed by up to two guests and lasts for 15 minutes.

There are four VIP areas at Mavericks, each with their own distinct ambience. The Platinum Lounge, Empire Lounge, The Mezzanine, and the VVIP Library. The Platinum VIP Lounge has leather chairs and couches centred around the VIP Birdcage stage, and walls lined with booths draped in thick red velvet curtains for privacy and discretion. The Empire Lounge centres around the bar and it’s attractive clientele. It extends to a private pole dancing stage. The atmosphere is more glamorous and social than the Platinum lounge, yet somewhat quieter than the main floor with discreet lap dancing booths. The Mezzanine is a VIP area overlooking the main floor’s entertainment stage, yet elevated with a birds eye view of the main entertainment and two stages. It has large booths for social gatherings and smaller tables for those looking to see and be seen, and soak up the venue’s buzzing ambience.
