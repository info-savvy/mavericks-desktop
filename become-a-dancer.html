Accommodation & Safety

Your Home, Right Next Door

When you arrive, the House Manager greets you, takes you to your modern apartment complex right beside the club, helps you settle in and introduces you to the other dancers and our support staff. Every effort is made to help you feel at home as quickly as possible — we’ll even show you around the city, help with shopping and a local SIM, and give you a Welcome Pack.

Mavericks’ dancers live in an upmarket, fully-furnished complex to a high standard, with security as the top priority.

  • Fully furnished bedrooms & living rooms
  • Two fitted kitchens & laundry facilities
  • Satellite TV, sound systems & fast internet
  • Bar fridge & personal electronic safe in every room
  • Daily housekeeping service
  • Rooftop pool deck & tanning area with Table Mountain views
  • 24-hour security guard, controlled access & CCTV
  • Walking distance to nightlife, gyms, parks & shopping

Two to three dancers per room at ZAR 1,300 (± $85) per dancer, per week. Discounted meals are available at the club’s restaurant every evening.

Good to Know

The Essentials

Working Hours & Contract

Mavericks is open Monday to Saturday, 7pm–4am. Dancers work 5 nights a week with 2 nights off. We recommend staying a minimum of 3 months.

Dress Code

Elegant yet sexy at all times. Long and short dresses and see-through numbers on the floor; costumes and lingerie on stage; high heels and boots welcome. Change outfits at least once an evening — our resident stylists help you shine.

Weekly Levy & Accommodation

A weekly levy of R4,300 (± $270) covers dancing at the club and your accommodation. Please arrive with enough to cover your basic living costs during your first days.

Airfares

Flights are arranged and paid by you — Mavericks does not sponsor or credit the purchase of airfares.

Visas

Visa requirements depend on your country of residence. We’ll talk you through your options once we’ve received your application.

Fast, Fair Process

Your application is handled within 48 hours by Mavericks management. We never charge agency fees. Questions? Email danceradministrator@mavericks.co.za.
