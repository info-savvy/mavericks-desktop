VIP Membership

All guests to Mavericks Cape Town Gentlemen’s Club experience stimulating entertainment, sensual dances and pure indulgence in a luxurious environment. Mavericks offers a gentleman various levels of entertainment.

To access our VIP lounges in style — with friends or visiting business clients — simply join the Mavericks Membership Club, and enjoy absolute luxury and the very best the club has to offer.
